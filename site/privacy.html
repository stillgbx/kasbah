Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy covers Kasbah, the Android and Android Auto app published on Google Play (package org.kasbah.auto). Kasbah is open-source software: the app's code is public in the GitHub repository, so everything below can be checked.

In short

  • The app talks only to your own Kasbah server, at the address you type in. The publisher runs no server, and receives nothing from the app.
  • No account with the publisher, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking.
  • Your position stays on the phone.

What the app keeps on the phone

  • Your server's address and your user name on it.
  • The session cookie your server gives back when you sign in. Your password is sent once, to your server, to sign in, and is never stored.
  • Your house's position, read from your server, to place the approach alert (see below).
  • Your display choices: the order of the gates and doors, and which ones the approach alert offers.

Signing out closes the session on your server and forgets it on the phone. Uninstalling the app erases all of the above.

Location

The app asks for your location, including in the background, for a single feature: the alert that offers to open the gate or the garage door as you near home. It places a geofence around your house with Android's location services (Google Play services), which wake the app when you enter or leave that area. The app does not send your position to your server, to the publisher or to anyone else. Without the background location, everything else works; only the alert is missing.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you tap Talk to Kasbah in the car. The sentence you say is sent to your Kasbah server, which turns it into text with the transcription service its owner configured (on the server itself, or an online service of their choice), then hands it to the server's assistant. Nothing is recorded outside of that button.

Car data

The Car screen, on the car's display, shows what the car shares with apps: its model, the battery or fuel level, the speed, the odometer and the position. These values are displayed on that screen only: they are not stored, and not sent anywhere.

Notifications

Notifications are created on the phone by the app itself, for the approach alert. They go through no outside service.

Your server

Your Kasbah server is run by you (or by whoever invited you to it), not by the publisher. What it does with your requests depends on how its owner set it up — for example, which vendor services it uses for your devices, and which transcription or assistant service it calls. Those services have their own policies.

Children

Kasbah is a family app, and children may use it too. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included: everything described above stays on the phone or goes to the family's own Kasbah server.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. Its history is in the repository.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.

This website

This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites, except the YouTube video on the home page, and only once you click it (through youtube-nocookie.com). It opens in French when your browser asks for French; if you use the EN / FR switch, your browser remembers that choice (and nothing else) in its local storage. It is hosted by GitHub Pages.

The demo runs entirely in your browser, on simulated devices: it sends nothing to the publisher or to any server of yours. It keeps its own choices (the property shown, the theme, the language…) in your browser's local storage. Its maps and weather radar load their images and data from tiles.openfreemap.org, data.geopf.fr (IGN), rainviewer.com and Open-Meteo (openmeteo.s3.amazonaws.com), which see your IP address like any website you visit; nothing else leaves the page.
