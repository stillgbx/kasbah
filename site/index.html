Your whole home, in 3D, in one app

Kasbah shows your house and garden in 3D and lets you see and control the connected devices in it — lights, shutters, speakers, cameras, heating, solar, cars, robots, pet feeders and fountains — from many brands, side by side.

Features

One house, every device, wherever you are

Many brands

Your devices, side by side

Hue lights, Somfy shutters, Sonos and Cast speakers, Nest cameras, Enphase solar, Tesla and Renault cars, robot mowers and vacuums, pet feeders and fountains. Each vendor is a plugin on your server, so the app only knows lights, covers and players — and the API keys never reach the browser.

3D

Your house in 3D, inside too

Floors, rooms, doors and windows, the garden and the pool are drawn from a single JSON file. Cut away a level to look inside, tap a room, then a device. One finger turns the house, two fingers zoom.

Weather, sun, seasons

The real sky above your roof

The sun follows the house's GPS position and the clock. Rain and snow fall slanted by the wind, the ground gets wet, fog rolls in, the pool ripples with the breeze, and the garden changes with the seasons. Forecasts, air quality and pollen have their own view.

Music

Music that follows you

Search YouTube Music, Mixcloud or thousands of radio stations, build a queue, and play it in the browser. Then send it on to a Cast or Sonos speaker, or to the TV.

Your AI, through MCP

Talk to your house

Kasbah is an MCP server with its own sign-in. Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Mistral, Grok or a local LLM, and ask them to close the shutters or check the car's charge. A built-in assistant, “Ask Kasbah”, answers in the app, and out loud if you like.

Everywhere

Tablet, computer, phone, car

A web app that installs on any screen, with push notifications for the house's alerts. In the car, the Android Auto app opens the gate and the garage doors, offers to open them as you near home, and takes voice instructions.

Make it yours

Themes and 3D styles

Six themes in light and dark — Kasbah, Catppuccin, Dracula, Night Owl, Tokyo Night, Synthwave '84 — and an appearance that can follow the sun. Thirteen rendering styles for the house, from realistic to cyberpunk. In English and French.

Get started

Up and running in four commands

You need Node 22 or later and git. With no configuration at all, Kasbah starts on three demo houses, populated by the simulation.

git clone https://github.com/stillgbx/kasbah.git && cd kasbah
npm install
cp apps/server/.env.example apps/server/.env   # then add your devices' settings
npm run dev

Production, remote access, AI assistants and each plugin's setup: see the README.
